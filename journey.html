What I Am Working Towards

Clear goals keep study hours from turning into endless scrolling. These are mine for the next phase.

Score well in MHT-CET

My main target this year. Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology — with speed practice so the paper never feels too long.

Keep board marks strong

Entrance exams are built on the board syllabus. Strong HSC marks and a good entrance score together open the most doors.

Attempt more than one exam

NEET, JEE Main and CUET as well. Different patterns, different strengths tested — and more than one chance to get it right.

Fix the weak chapters first

An honest error log instead of comfortable revision. The chapters I avoid are exactly the ones costing me marks.

Get into a good college

A course that fits my strengths in science — and a campus where studying hard is the normal thing to do.

I do not need to be the best in the room. I just need to be better than I was last month.

— Naitik Sushil Warjurkar
