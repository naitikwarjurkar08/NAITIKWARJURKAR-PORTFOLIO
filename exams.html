How I prepare

My Study Loop

Four steps, repeated for every subject and every exam — no shortcuts, no fancy system.

Read the Syllabus

Write down exactly which chapters are in and which are out, so no study hour is wasted on the wrong topic.

Make Short Notes

One page per chapter in my own words — formulas, definitions and the mistakes I already know I make.

Solve Papers

Previous year questions and full mocks under real time limits. Speed only comes from actually writing papers.

Review Mistakes

The error log matters more than the score. Every repeated mistake is a mark I am giving away for free.

One exam does not decide a student's future. Trying again, with better preparation, always stays an option.

— Naitik Sushil Warjurkar
